Questions before you book.

How fast will I get a price?

Usually within 15 minutes, Monday to Saturday. For urgent loads call 0800 123 4567 and we price while you are on the line.

Is the price really fixed?

Yes. It includes fuel, tolls and standard waiting time. We only change it if the load or addresses change.

What if goods are damaged or late?

Every load carries up to £2m goods-in-transit cover. If we are late through our own fault, 20% is credited automatically.

Can I set up regular collections?

Yes. Regular lanes get a locked rate, a named dispatcher and monthly invoicing on 30-day terms.
